To request a refund, please contact our support team at info@forgelogiclab.com or call us at +1 (302) 280-1080. Please include your registered account email and the Paddle transaction ID. Our team, in cooperation with Paddle, will review your request and respond within 3-5 business days. Once approved, refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 5-10 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Cases

To prevent abuse of our high-performance infrastructure, refunds will not be granted if you request them after extensively using the API limits, or if your account is terminated due to a violation of our Acceptable Use Policy and Terms of Service.
