What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored securely on your device by your web browser when you visit our website.

How we use them:

  • Essential Cookies: Strictly necessary to keep the site functional, manage user sessions securely, and process payments safely via Paddle.
  • Analytics Cookies: To understand how users interact with our tools, allowing us to improve performance and user interface design.
  • Preferences Cookies: To remember your language preferences and UI settings for a better, personalized experience.

You can manage, restrict, or disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. However, please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of your FileWizardIO account. For further questions, please reach out to us at info@forgelogiclab.com.
