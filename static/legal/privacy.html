This Privacy Policy applies to all services operated by ForgeLogic LLC, including FileWizardIO (filewizardio.com) and SwapForge (AI face-swap mobile application).

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe, Inc. — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
