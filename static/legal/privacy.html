Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

At FileWizardIO, we prioritize your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

We do not require user registration. We do not store the original files (images, PDFs) you upload. Files are processed in temporary server memory and deleted immediately after the session or within 1 hour.

2. Cookies and Ads

We use third-party advertising services (like Google AdSense). These services may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

3. Data Security

All transfers between your browser and our servers are encrypted via SSL (HTTPS).
