SubTrack – Privacy Policy

SubTrack: Subscription Tracker · Package: com.forgelogic.subtrack · Developer: ForgeLogic Apps
Last updated: 27 July 2026

1. Overview

SubTrack ("the app", "we", "us") is a subscription-tracking application developed by ForgeLogic Apps. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short: SubTrack does not require an account, does not track you, and keeps your data on your own device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We operate no backend server, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs.

Data you enter (stored only on your device)

  • Subscription details you add (name, price, billing cycle, dates, notes).
  • App preferences and premium status.

This information is stored locally in the app's private database on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

Premium features can be unlocked through an optional one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play in accordance with Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see or store your payment card details.

Company Logos (Google Favicon Service)

To display a logo next to each subscription, the app requests an icon from Google's public favicon service (google.com/s2/favicons) using only the website domain derived from the subscription name (for example, "netflix.com"). No personal identifier is sent with this request and the request is not linked to your identity.

Notifications

The app can show local reminder notifications on your device. These are generated on-device and are not sent to any server.

Biometric Lock (optional)

You may optionally protect the app with your device's biometric lock (fingerprint / face). This is handled entirely by Android on your device; the app never accesses or stores your biometric data.

4. Permissions

  • Internet – to load company logos and process in-app purchases.
  • Notifications – to show subscription reminders.
  • Biometric – for the optional app lock.
  • Run at startup – to reschedule reminders after the device restarts.
  • Billing – for the optional premium in-app purchase.

5. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for advertising or any other purpose. Because your data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All app data lives only on your device for as long as the app is installed. To delete everything, simply uninstall SubTrack or clear the app's storage in your device settings.

7. Children

SubTrack is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.
